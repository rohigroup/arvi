# ARVI pricing architecture

ARVI distinguishes productized verticals from broader automation implementations.

## Productized entry products
- ARVI Agenda Esencial: COP $490.000 + IVA implementation; COP $199.000/month + IVA operation.
- ARVI Agenda Inteligente: COP $890.000 + IVA implementation; COP $349.000/month + IVA operation.
- Agenda availability remains deterministic: services, durations, professionals, schedules, blocks, existing appointments and business rules decide valid slots.

## Broader automation
- ARVI Inicio: COP $1.690.000 + IVA implementation; operation from COP $179.000/month + IVA.
- ARVI Pro: COP $4.490.000 + IVA implementation; operation from COP $449.000/month + IVA.
- ARVI a medida: from COP $8.990.000 + IVA implementation; operation from COP $799.000/month + IVA.

Do not collapse ARVI Agenda into ARVI Pro. Agenda is intentionally standardized and lower-cost so it can be replicated across appointment-based businesses.
